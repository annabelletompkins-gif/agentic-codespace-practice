# 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968)

**Director:** Stanley Kubrick
**Genre:** Science fiction

A mysterious black monolith appears at key moments in human history, guiding our species from bone-wielding apes to a mission toward Jupiter. Along the way, the ship's calmly murderous computer HAL 9000 turns on its crew. A slow, hypnotic film more interested in questions than answers, famous for its match-cut from a thrown bone to an orbiting station.
