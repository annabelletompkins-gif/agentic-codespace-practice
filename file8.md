# The Matrix (1999)

**Director:** The Wachowskis
**Genre:** Science fiction / action

Hacker Neo learns the world he knows is a simulation built to keep humanity docile while machines harvest their bodies. Freed by a rebel crew, he is taught to bend the rules of the fake reality and becomes the reluctant hope of a war against the machines. Famous for bullet-time, green code, and the red-pill choice.
