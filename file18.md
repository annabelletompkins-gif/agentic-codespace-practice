# The Dark Knight (2008)

**Director:** Christopher Nolan
**Genre:** Superhero / crime thriller

Batman, police, and mobsters are thrown into chaos by the Joker, an agent of pure anarchy who wants only to prove that order is a lie. The film turns a comic-book premise into a tense crime epic about escalation, compromise, and how far a good man will go. A defining performance anchors its center.
