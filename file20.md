# Toy Story (1995)

**Director:** John Lasseter
**Genre:** Animated family

Cowboy doll Woody's comfortable place as a boy's favorite toy is upended by Buzz Lightyear, a spaceman who doesn't realize he's a toy. Stranded together, the rivals must cooperate to get home. The first fully computer-animated feature, and still a tender story about friendship, jealousy, and growing older.
