# Spirited Away (2001)

**Director:** Hayao Miyazaki
**Genre:** Animated fantasy

Ten-year-old Chihiro stumbles into a world of spirits after her parents are turned into pigs. Forced to work in a bathhouse run by the witch Yubaba, she must remember her own name to free herself and her family. A luminous, hand-drawn adventure full of strange creatures, quiet courage, and growing up.
