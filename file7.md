# Pulp Fiction (1994)

**Director:** Quentin Tarantino
**Genre:** Crime

Two hitmen, a boxer, a gangster's wife, and a pair of diner robbers cross paths in a story told out of order. Dialogue-heavy, cool, and violent, the film reshaped independent cinema in the 1990s with its chapter structure, needle-drops, and endlessly imitated banter about burgers and briefcases.
