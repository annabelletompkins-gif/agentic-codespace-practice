# The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring (2001)

**Director:** Peter Jackson
**Genre:** Epic fantasy

A hobbit named Frodo inherits a ring that could doom the world, and sets out with a diverse fellowship to destroy it in the fires of Mordor. The first chapter of the trilogy builds Middle-earth in loving detail, from the green hills of the Shire to the dark mines beneath the mountains, and ends on a note of parting and resolve.
