# The Shawshank Redemption (1994)

**Director:** Frank Darabont
**Genre:** Drama

Wrongly convicted banker Andy Dufresne is sentenced to life at Shawshank State Penitentiary for a murder he did not commit. Over two decades he forms a quiet friendship with fellow inmate Red, endures the cruelty of the warden, and slowly plots something the guards never see coming. A story about hope, patience, and the human refusal to be broken.
