# Blade Runner (1982)

**Director:** Ridley Scott
**Genre:** Neo-noir science fiction

In a rain-soaked future Los Angeles, a burnt-out cop hunts four escaped replicants — synthetic humans seeking more life. The deeper he goes, the harder it is to tell hunter from hunted, and the film's villains become its most human figures. A moody meditation on memory, mortality, and what it means to be alive.
