# Casablanca (1942)

**Director:** Michael Curtiz
**Genre:** Romance / wartime drama

In the Moroccan city of Casablanca during World War II, a cynical nightclub owner named Rick runs into the woman who once left him. She needs exit papers to escape the Nazis, and Rick must choose between love and doing the right thing. A film built on fog, piano keys, and one of cinema's most quoted farewells.
